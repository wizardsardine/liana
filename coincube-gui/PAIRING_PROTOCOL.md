Pairing completion protocol 1 (within QR v2)

The QR PSK, TLS identity, exact selected xpub/origin and descriptor commitment checks precede staging. PairingComplete.completion_protocol must equal 1. Pong is exclusively liveness. Old peers must update both applications and create a fresh offer.

Desktop stages an immutable candidate and exact prior row under a new random transaction ID, then sends ACCEPT. Phone stages the same authenticated binding and prior row, then replies PREPARED. Neither stage is a signing capability. Public store reads mask unfinished candidates with the prior row.

Desktop writes its hidden candidate under the cancellation lock, then sends COMMIT; phone writes its hidden candidate and replies COMMITTED. Phone storage refusal is read by desktop and rolls back. Cancel remains effective through this exchange. Only after COMMITTED does the desktop serialize its durable completion decision with Cancel: if Cancel wins, the candidate is rolled back; if the completion write wins, both candidates are already durable and subsequent Cancel/navigation cannot reverse that decision. Desktop then sends FINISH and waits for FINISHED. Phone marks its row finished only after FINISH, sends FINISHED, and reports success. Desktop reports success only after FINISHED. Each side therefore knows both candidate writes completed before success.

All post-staging protocol failures are terminal, requiring a fresh user-approved offer, never automatic redial with a consumed offer. Known refusal/unexpected frame/storage error restores prior rows. Desktop loss of a final response leaves its candidate hidden/pending. Phone loss before completing FINISH restores its prior row. Every desktop journal operation checks certificate AND its locally generated transaction ID. The phone uses a separate locally generated 256-bit nonce for journal ownership; the peer-selected wire ID only sequences messages. Reusing a wire ID cannot give old cleanup ownership of a new phone attempt. Supersession, rename and unpair revoke old journal ownership. An abandoned pending attempt is recovered by an explicit fresh pairing; staging restores the prior row and replaces the old ID. Late cleanup cannot affect the replacement.

This is not perfectly atomic distributed commit. If FINISHED is lost after phone success, phone can have a completed row while desktop retains a hidden pending row. A process can also die after its durable local finish and before delivery. There is no atomic transaction across two independent stores and a socket. The desktop cannot use its hidden candidate for signing; recovering requires explicit re-pair/update, not accepting a liveness packet as a commit. A persistent disk/keychain failure can also prevent rollback itself; unfinished journals continue masking candidates, and the error is reported. Physical storage corruption or loss of a journal is outside this crash/transport model.
